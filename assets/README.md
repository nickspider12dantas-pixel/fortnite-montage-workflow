# Montage music asset

## Suggested free track

Try [Big Bitty (Techno | Dubstep | EDM | Electronic Music) by firemansuperhero on Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/electronic-big-bitty-techno-dubstep-edm-electronic-music-121803/). Its track page describes it as energetic electronic music and says the composer releases music under a Creative Commons Attribution license with no Content ID. The page also says to credit FIREMAN. This is a candidate, not a guarantee against TikTok muting or copyright claims: re-check the live track page and applicable license before publishing.

Official [Pixabay Content License summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).

## Add the music

1. Download the MP3 from the official track page.
2. Add it to this repository at `assets/music.mp3`.
3. Edit `music.json` and enter the track title, artist, source URL, license, and required credit in `license_note`.
4. Keep a copy of the track page and license details.

Do not upload a track unless its current license permits use in your TikTok montage. The workflow uses a local MP3; it does not need a public direct music URL.
